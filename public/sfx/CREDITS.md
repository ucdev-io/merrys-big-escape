# Sound credits

The recorded sounds in this folder are short cuts from CC0 recordings on [freesound.org](https://freesound.org)
(public domain: free for anyone to use, no credit needed). They were trimmed, made mono, levelled and saved as
small MP3s for the game. Thank you to everyone who recorded and shared them!
